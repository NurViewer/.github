## Why NurViewer?

Digital files come in hundreds of different formats. Reading, searching, annotating, converting, scanning, and managing them often requires multiple applications.

We are developing NurViewer to bring these everyday tasks together in one application.

Our goal is simple: fewer applications, fewer interruptions, and more time for the work that matters.

## 1. Open 600+ File Formats

NurViewer supports a wide range of file formats and extensions, including:

- Ebooks: EPUB, MOBI, AZW3 and more
- Scanned books and academic documents: PDG, CAJ, DjVu
- Documents: PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and more
- Images: JPEG, PNG, TIFF, HEIC, PSD and camera RAW
- Archives: ZIP, RAR, 7Z and more
- Audio and video files
- Dictionaries: MDict, StarDict and more
- Specialist files: DICOM, 3D models and data files

Available capabilities vary by format and platform, from full reading and playback to preview and metadata inspection.

[Explore the complete format catalogue](https://nursova.com/nurviewer/formats/)

## Privacy and Local Processing

NurViewer is designed around local file processing.

Supported document viewing, recognition, searching and conversion tasks are performed on your device.

Source documents are not uploaded to our account service during ordinary local processing.

[Privacy Policy](https://nursova.com/nurviewer/privacy/)
